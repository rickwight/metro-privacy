Metro privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy covers Metro, the metronome app for Android published on Google Play by Rick Wight (package name com.rickwight.metro).

Summary

Metro does not collect, store, or share any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data on your device

The songs, playlists, tempo maps, and settings you create are saved only in Metro's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. They are not included in Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling Metro, or using Clear library in its settings, deletes them.

Import and export

When you export your library, Metro writes a file to a location you choose in Android's file picker. When you import, Metro reads only the file you pick. What happens to an exported file afterwards, such as sharing it or saving it to a cloud drive, is up to you and the app or service you use for that.

Permissions

Metro does not ask for any permissions. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information.

Third parties

Metro contains no third-party services that collect data. Google Play may collect information when you download and update the app, under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Metro collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
